## Break-even accounting contract

`PL-BE` must keep units explicit. Its minimum executable model is:

```text
per-run delta =
  control complete-work time
  - candidate complete-work time
  - candidate incremental allocation/GC time

startup delta =
  candidate package parse/load time
  - control package parse/load time

break-even runs =
  (one-time migration and validation cost + positive startup delta)
  / positive per-run delta
```
