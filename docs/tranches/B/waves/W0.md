- production file bounds expand beyond the named generic runtime files;
- equal-result or ≥10× CI-low fails for a third distinct design iteration;
- CSS-specific types, trivia, recovery or registry machinery appears in
  parse-that;

  alias;

  cause.

   the same evidence before formation admission.

   the frozen standards denominator and named extensions with WPT/browser

9. Both consumer diffs prove net deletion/consolidation; no token/scanner,
   serializer, alias, shim or fallback path remains.
10. At every declared scale, the exact-bootstrap 95% low is ≥10× for equal
   values, spans, diagnostics, recovery and failure behavior. Result
   materialization is timed, not excluded.
11. CPU, heap, allocation, hidden-class/IC, optimization/deoptimization and
